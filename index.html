About the Model

Powered by Machine Learning

Clustering Algorithm

CropWise uses a Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) trained on extensive agricultural data covering 22 crop types. The model identifies natural groupings in soil and climate patterns to match your conditions with the most suitable crops.

22 Crop Types

From staple grains like rice and maize, to pulses like chickpea and lentil, to fruits like mango and watermelon — the model covers a diverse range of Indian agricultural crops across all major seasons.

7 Input Features

The prediction pipeline considers Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, soil pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall — the key factors that determine crop viability in any given region.
